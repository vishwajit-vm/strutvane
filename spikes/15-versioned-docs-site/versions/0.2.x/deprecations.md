Before anything is removed from Strutvane, it is deprecated for at least one full line: your editor shows it struck through, and at runtime you get one warning per process with a stable `STRUTVANE_DEPRECATED_…` code. This page will list every deprecation in 0.2, generated from the framework's deprecation registry.
