---
title: "The Sidebar"
description: "How generated resources appear in the admin sidebar, and how to change their label, icon, group and order."
---

One sidebar entry per resource, with its plural label, an icon, a group, an order and an optional badge. Entries are hidden from people who may not `viewAny` that resource.
